# Unit 18 — Premium product redesign

## Scope

Refresh the experimental VantageForge popup and dashboard into a calm, chart-first personal workspace without changing capture, storage, review, search, analytics, or local-AI contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

- Normal VantageForge branding is used throughout the UI.
- Dashboard has persistent Today/Trades/Patterns/Experiments navigation and visible Local & private status.
- Today framing prioritises attention and review action before raw metrics.
- Trade cards remain screenshot-first, newest-first, keyboard accessible, and retain delete behavior.
- Review modal remains near-full-screen, chart-first, responsive, and preserves all existing IDs and actions.
- Popup remains focused on one primary Capture Trade action.
- Reduced-motion and responsive states are supported.
