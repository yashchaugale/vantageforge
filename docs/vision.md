# Vision

## Mission

Build the simplest AI-powered trading companion that helps discretionary traders improve consistently without the burden of manual journaling.

---

## Vision

Trading journals should disappear into the background.

A trader should focus on making better decisions—not documenting them.

VantageForge automatically captures, understands, and organizes every trade, then turns that information into personalized insights that improve discipline, execution, and consistency.

---

## Product Principles

- Zero Friction > More Features
- Automation First
- Local First
- Privacy by Default
- AI Explains, Not Commands
- Simplicity Wins
- Every Feature Must Improve Decision Making

---

## Success

A trader closes a trade.

One click later, everything is captured.

Later, instead of filling spreadsheets, they review what actually matters:

- What went wrong
- What improved
- What patterns are repeating
- What should change next
