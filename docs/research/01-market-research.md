# Market Research

## Objective

Understand the current trading journal market, identify common user problems, and determine whether there is room for a new product.

---

# Current Market

Trading journals have become significantly more advanced over the last few years.

Modern platforms now provide:

- Automatic broker imports
- Performance analytics
- AI-generated summaries
- Trade replay
- Psychology tracking
- Rule tracking
- Multi-account support
- Risk metrics
- Dashboard reporting

Leading products include:

- TradeZella
- TraderSync
- TradesViz
- Tradervue

This means the market is already mature.

Competing by simply adding features is not a viable strategy.

---

# Market Trends

The industry is moving toward:

- AI-assisted journaling
- Automatic trade imports
- Psychology tracking
- Performance dashboards
- Cloud-based platforms
- Subscription business models

Artificial Intelligence is no longer a unique selling point.

It has become an expected feature.

---

# What Existing Products Do Well

Current products successfully solve:

- Data collection
- Performance reporting
- Broker synchronization
- Trade organization
- Statistical analysis
- Historical record keeping

These problems are considered largely solved.

---

# Common User Frustrations

Research across Reddit, reviews, and community discussions revealed recurring themes.

## 1. Subscription Cost

Many traders consider monthly subscriptions expensive, especially those who are not consistently profitable.

---

## 2. Feature Overload

Several users describe modern journals as complex and difficult to navigate.

More features do not always create more value.

---

## 3. Inconsistent Journaling

Many traders begin journaling with enthusiasm but stop after a few weeks.

The problem is not awareness.

The problem is consistency.

---

## 4. Manual Work

Discretionary traders often maintain multiple tools:

TradingView

↓

Screenshots

↓

Notion

↓

Excel

↓

Discord

↓

Voice Notes

↓

Weekly Review

Context becomes fragmented.

---

## 5. Statistics Without Action

Many traders receive dashboards but still struggle to answer:

"What should I improve tomorrow?"

Information alone does not guarantee improvement.

---

# Market Opportunity

Rather than competing on feature count, an opportunity exists to build software focused on:

- Simplicity
- Automation
- Low-friction workflows
- AI-assisted reflection
- Daily consistency

The opportunity is not to replace every existing journal.

The opportunity is to create a product traders actually continue using.

---

# Key Insight

The biggest challenge is no longer collecting trading data.

The challenge is helping traders consistently learn from it.

---

# Conclusion

The trading journal market is mature and competitive.

However, there is still room for products that prioritize:

- Better workflow
- Better user experience
- Reduced manual effort
- Consistent daily usage

VantageForge will compete on execution, simplicity, and automation rather than feature count.
