# Monetization Plan

## Personal edition

Local SQLite is free to run. Users supply optional Notion and cloud-AI credentials. Do not promise unlimited third-party storage or permanently free model inference.

## Future paid edition

Potential paid value is the polished laboratory workflow, hosted sync, secure backups, deterministic discovery, memory, experiments, and optional managed AI—not raw LLM access alone. Keep a useful local/free tier and disclose provider costs.
